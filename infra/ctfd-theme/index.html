TRISS CTF

How it works

Find the flag, score the points

Each challenge hides a flag. Paste it into the challenge window to score. Some hints are free and some cost points, so weigh it up before you open one.

  • Beginner

    100 points

    Start here. One idea, a nudge in the description, and hints that walk you to the answer.

  • Intermediate

    250 points

    Two or three steps, and you'll need a tool or a short script.

  • Advanced

    500 points

    The hard ones. Expect to read code, write code, and take your time.

  • Flag format

    BarSides{...}

    Submit the whole thing, braces and all. Flags are case sensitive.

House rules

Play nice

  • Attack the challenges, not the CTF server, the network, or other players.
  • Don't share flags or answers with other players.
  • Solve the challenges yourself: please don't use AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot to solve them. (The chatbots in the AI category are fair game to attack.)
  • Stuck, or think something is broken? Find a BarSides organizer at the CTF table.
